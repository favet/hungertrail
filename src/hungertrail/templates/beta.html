How to play

You are a tribute. 23 others want you dead. Last one alive wins.

  • ❤️ Health: hit zero and you're out.
  • 🎒 Supplies: days of food and water. You use one every night. Run out and you starve.
  • Kit: three slots. Tap the medkit to use it. Weapons don't stack: only your best one counts in a fight. When you find more than fits, you choose what to keep on one screen.
  • ⭐ Stars: the crowd's favor. Long shots, kills, the cameras and sharing what you can't carry earn them. Walking away costs one. Spend them on a sponsor parachute that drops at night, or at once in the final fight.
  • Every morning, choose where to go. Each place gives you something and risks something, and deals you what happens there.
  • Every choice shows its stakes: the odds, ✓ what you get if it works, ✗ what it costs if it doesn't. ☠️ means it could kill you.
  • The edge of the arena is quiet. Stay there too long and the Gamemakers come for you. From day 6 it's gone.
  • One Career is the Capitol's favorite killer. Same one for everyone today. You'll meet.
  • Some things you play, not roll: 🎣 fishing at the stream, 🏹 hunting on the ridge, 🤫 holding still when someone comes looking.
  • The dig at the horn is push your luck. Every noise costs a little; the third brings a Career, and you drop everything and run.
  • The final fight runs on health, yours and theirs. Watch which side they wind up on, block it, then strike. Your weapon makes the strike longer.

One ranked run a day: your first. Replays of today's arena and practice runs are just for you.
