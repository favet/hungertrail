How to play

You are a tribute. 23 others want you dead. Survive about eight days and win the final fight.

  • Each day brings an event, then a choice at camp before you sleep.
  • Odds are shown on risky choices. Your health, gear, trait and allies change them.
  • Food and water run down every night. Run out and you'll pay in health.
  • Favor is what sponsors think of you. Earn enough and a silver parachute arrives at night.
  • Heat is the Gamemakers' boredom. Hide too long and they'll come for you with fire and teeth.
  • Cannons fire when tributes fall. At night their faces appear in the sky.
  • Score comes from placement, kills and days survived, times your district multiplier. Careers are easy (×1). District 11 and 12 are hard (×3).

One ranked run a day. Practice as much as you like.
